Understanding Microplastics

🏭

Sources

Microplastics come from various sources including synthetic clothing fibers, cosmetic products, tire wear, and the breakdown of larger plastic items.

🐟

Environmental Impact

These tiny particles are consumed by marine life, entering the food chain and potentially affecting human health through seafood consumption.

🔬

Detection

Scientists use advanced techniques like spectroscopy and microscopy to identify and quantify microplastics in water samples.
